## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, precise, and transparent operational environment for institutional research asset governance. Rooted in university identity, it communicates rigorous stewardship, scientific credibility, and computational clarity. The visual demeanor balances institutional gravitas with the streamlined convenience of high-end enterprise procurement and digital cataloging suites.

The primary target audience comprises principal investigators, laboratory heads, academic researchers, procurement officers, and inventory auditors. The interface balances high information density with immediate legibility, allowing users to rapidly scan serial numbers, operational statuses, calibration records, and booking schedules without cognitive friction.

Drawing from **Modern Corporate & Editorial Catalog** design philosophies, the style relies on:
- Architectonic layout modules with fine hairline borders.
- Crisp, high-contrast typography emphasizing systematic metadata readability.
- Rich, deep institutional blues counterbalanced by functional golden-amber highlights and semantic health badges.
- Deliberate reduction of visual noise, prioritizing tabular hygiene, asset specimen imagery, and clear action hierarchies.

## Colors

The color architecture directly mirrors institutional heritage, utilizing the university's signature blue and warm golden emblem tones while maintaining strict WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility ratios across all data surfaces.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0d4a8e`):** The signature university blue, deployed for primary actions, navigational sidebars, active tabs, header structures, and authoritative brand anchor points.
- **Secondary (`#f5a623`):** Vibrant amber-gold from the university core mark, reserved for key focal interactions, reservation highlights, actionable notifications, and intermediate warning states.
- **Tertiary (`#0a386b`):** Deep navy indigo, utilized for high-contrast interactive states (hover/active), top-level breadcrumb paths, and dense analytical charts.
- **Neutral Base (`#0f172a`):** Deep slate-black, ensuring absolute clarity for primary metadata, asset designations, and serial IDs.

### Semantic & Surface Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) provides a soft, eye-resting foundation that eliminates sterile glare during extended auditing shifts.
- **Card & Data Panels:** `#ffffff` (Pure White) with 1px border outlines (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Secondary Surfaces & Table Striations:** `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100).
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** `#64748b` (Slate 500) and `#334155` (Slate 700).
- **Status Green (Tersedia / Kondisi Baik):** `#10b981` (Surface: `#ecfdf5`, Text: `#065f46`).
- **Status Amber (Dipinjam / Pemeliharaan):** `#f59e0b` (Surface: `#fffbeb`, Text: `#92400e`).
- **Status Rose (Rusak / Butuh Perbaikan):** `#f43f5e` (Surface: `#fff1f2`, Text: `#9f1239`).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to infuse geometric structure, modern academic authority, and clean open counters for technical alphanumeric codes. 

### Scale Applications
- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`):** Used strictly for high-level dashboard summaries, lab directory headers, and asset master view titles.
- **Titles (`title-md`, `title-sm`):** Govern catalog card labels, table section breaks, modal titles, and KPI card categorizations.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** Structured for dense descriptions, specification sheets, maintenance logs, and research user audit logs. 
- **Labels & Microcopy (`label-md`, `label-sm`):** Capitalized with `0.04em` tracking for status pills, category taxonomies, table column headers, and filter badges.
- **Code & Serial Numerals:** When rendering barcode values, inventory tags, and BMN (Barang Milik Negara) registration strings, text styles should apply tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to prevent vertical jitter across tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a **12-column responsive fluid grid** pinned to a maximum canvas constraint of `1440px` for wide laboratory administration workstations.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with fixed 260px administrative navigation drawer on the left. Primary content viewport uses `2rem` margin padding and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Collapsible sidebar rail (72px collapsed). Grid reflows into 6 columns; catalog item grids adjust from 4 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Side navigation collapses into an off-canvas drawer. Gutters collapse to `0.75rem` and outer margins shrink to `1rem` to maximize screen real estate.

### Spacing Cadence
- Form inputs, tabular rows, and interactive chips use compact vertical spacing (`space-sm`, `space-md`) to ensure critical research data remains visible above the fold.
- Asset catalog cards adopt `space-md` interior padding, ensuring equipment thumbnails and specs remain clearly segregated.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, blurred drop shadows to maintain a clean corporate catalog feel. Depth is achieved via **tonal stratification and sharp hairline borders**.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas (`#f8fafc`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Table Containers):** Pure white background (`#ffffff`) bounded by a subtle border: `1px solid #e2e8f0`. No blur shadow; clarity is established purely through surface contrast.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Asset Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle ambient rise: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Asset Booking Drawers, Dialogs):** High-focus lift: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(13, 74, 142, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Supported by a darkened backdrop blur overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a disciplined **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape philosophy. This provides a balance between institutional reliability and contemporary digital product ergonomics.

- **Base Elements (`rounded` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Buttons, search inputs, dropdown filters, segmented controllers, and thumbnail frames.
- **Containers (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Asset cards, metric overview modules, data table parent enclosures, and analytical graphs.
- **Large Panels & Modals (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Equipment checkout sheets, schedule calendars, and master detail overlays.
- **Pills (`full-radius` = 9999px):** Strictly reserved for dynamic status indicators (e.g., *Kondisi Baik*, *Dipinjam*), user account tags, and small counter badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0d4a8e`, text `#ffffff`, border `none`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover: `#0a386b`. Active: transforms 1px downward.
- **Secondary / Action Accent:** Background `#f5a623`, text `#0f172a`, font-weight `600`. Reserved for checkout/pinjam workflows, reservations, and critical exports.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, text `#334155`. Hover: `#f1f5f9`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#fff1f2`, border `1px solid #fecdd3`, text `#e11d48`. Hover: `#ffe4e6`.

### Asset Cards (Catalog Grid)
- Designed like an editorial corporate product card:
  - Header with aspect-ratio 16:10 equipment photo or placeholder technical blueprint.
  - Floating pill badge in the upper right showing current asset state (e.g., *Tersedia* in Emerald Green).
  - Body containing Asset Name (`title-sm`), Faculty/Lab Sub-header (`body-sm` in Slate 500), and Inventory Serial Tag (monospace pill).
  - Footer with quick reservation CTA button and availability status dot.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Tersedia (Available):** Pill shape. Background `#ecfdf5`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`, leading pulse dot in `#10b981`.
- **Dipinjam (Borrowed / In Use):** Pill shape. Background `#fffbeb`, border `1px solid #fde68a`, text `#92400e`, leading dot in `#f59e0b`.
- **Pemeliharaan (Maintenance):** Pill shape. Background `#eff6ff`, border `1px solid #bfdbfe`, text `#1e40af`, leading dot in `#3b82f6`.
- **Rusak (Damaged / Out of Service):** Pill shape. Background `#fff1f2`, border `1px solid #fecdd3`, text `#9f1239`, leading dot in `#f43f5e`.

### Input Fields & Filter Controls
- **Search Bar:** Deep `#ffffff` container with `1px solid #cbd5e1`, leading magnifying glass icon in `#64748b`, height `42px`, padding `0 1rem`, placeholder `#94a3b8`. Focus state introduces a `2px` ring in `#0d4a8e` with an offset of `1px`.
- **Faceted Dropdown Filters:** Pill or rounded rectangle button showing selected value with chevron down. Active filter chips feature a subtle close button (`×`) to immediately clear parameters.

### Data Tables (Asset Master Records)
- Clean tabular hygiene. Header row in `#f8fafc` with uppercase `label-sm` text tracking.
- Row heights set to `52px` with horizontal hairlines `#f1f5f9`. 
- Alternating row zebra strips are avoided; instead, hover triggers subtle background highlight `#f8fafc`.
- Sticky right-most column for quick actions (Detail, Pinjam, QR Code Export).

### Stat KPI Cards
- White surface, `1px solid #e2e8f0` border, `1rem` radius.
- Top row: Metric label (`label-md` in Slate 500) paired with an icon tinted in either primary blue or secondary gold.
- Middle row: Large numerical count (`headline-lg` in `#0f172a`).
- Bottom row: Micro-trend line or operational status indicator (e.g., "+3 unit baru bulan ini").
